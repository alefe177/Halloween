IT

Horror / Supernatural

Movie Info

  • Year: 2017
  • Director: Andy Muschietti
  • Genre: Horror / Supernatural
  • Our rating: ★★★★★
  • Scare level: 4 out of 5 🎃
  • Best for: teens and adults

Synopsis

The film explores not only Pennywise's terror but also the friendship and growth of the Losers' Club. Despite bullying, personal struggles, and their fears, the seven friends support each other and unite to fight evil. It highlights the power of friendship, courage, and overcoming challenges.

Why We Recommend It

It is scary, but it is also a story about friendship and courage. The kids have to face their biggest fears together, and that makes the movie emotional too.
