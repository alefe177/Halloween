Monster House

Animation / Fantasy / Family

Movie Info

  • Year: 2006
  • Director: Gil Kenan
  • Genre: Animation / Fantasy / Family
  • Our rating: ★★★★★
  • Scare level: 1 out of 5 🎃
  • Best for: kids and families

Synopsis

Monster House is an animated movie about three young friends who discover that a mysterious house in their neighborhood is actually alive. As Halloween approaches, they notice strange things happening around the house and realize that it is dangerous. Together, they decide to investigate its secrets and find a way to stop it before anyone gets hurt. The movie is full of mystery, adventure, suspense, and Halloween spirit.

Why We Recommend It

It is spooky but not too scary, so it is perfect for a family night. It has humor, adventure, friendship and a surprising, sweet ending.
