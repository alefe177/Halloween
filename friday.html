Friday the 13th

Horror / Slasher

Movie Info

  • Year: 1980
  • Director: Sean S. Cunningham
  • Genre: Horror / Slasher
  • Our rating: ★★★★☆
  • Scare level: 4 out of 5 🎃
  • Best for: classic horror fans

Synopsis

A group of young counselors is getting Camp Crystal Lake ready to reopen. But someone is watching them from the woods, and one by one they disappear.

Why We Recommend It

It is a classic that helped create the slasher genre. If you want to understand where modern horror movies came from, you have to watch this one.
