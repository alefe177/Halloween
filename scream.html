Scream

Horror / Mystery

Movie Info

  • Year: 1996
  • Director: Wes Craven
  • Genre: Horror / Mystery / Slasher
  • Our rating: ★★★★★
  • Scare level: 4 out of 5 🎃
  • Best for: teens and friends

Synopsis

In the small town of Woodsboro, a series of murders begins to terrorize the residents. The killer, known as Ghostface, wears a terrifying mask and calls his victims before attacking them, asking questions about horror movies. Sidney Prescott, a young girl, becomes one of the killer's main targets. She tries to discover who is behind the murders, but everyone around her seems suspicious, including her own friends. What Attracts the Audience? What attracts people to watch Scream is the mystery surrounding the killer's identity, the suspenseful chases, unexpected scares, and surprising plot twists. Ghostface's mysterious identity makes viewers curious and keeps them watching until the end to discover who is behind the mask. Genre Horror, suspense and mystery. Director Wes Craven.

Why We Recommend It

It is smart and funny, and it makes fun of horror movie rules. You will try to guess the killer until the very last scene.
